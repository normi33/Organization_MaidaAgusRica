Who We Are

MS Guild is an organization of Muslim students at Palawan State University - Bataraza Campus. It promotes unity, friendship, and cooperation.

Our Mission

To promote unity, respect, teamwork, and cooperation among Muslim students.

Our Vision

To build a united community of students who value education, leadership, and culture.
